## Definition of detail-complete for A63 prototype layer

1. One continuous perceptual Field across arrival, accompaniment, Source, Territory and Tissue.
2. All families above have at least one navigable representative expression; no essential family remains documentation-only.
3. No universal card/renderer or organ-tab architecture is introduced.
4. LUMI availability is contextual and can withdraw to P0/P1; no dominant floating-chatbot dependency.
5. All experience forms have sovereign pause/exit and appropriate return behavior.
6. NO_MATCH, uncertainty and safety are first-class integrity expressions.
7. Memory and proactivity are visibly consented, reason-visible, correctable/forgettable/off-able.
8. Territory interactions preserve present-over-history and no productivity semantics.
9. Tissue keeps another Life central and includes limits/consent/safety/exit/report where material.
10. Source demonstrates heterogeneous renderers and constellational movement, not catalog consumption.
11. Accessibility/reduced-motion/transcript/caption/keyboard semantics survive the sensory identity.
12. Coverage is mapped to V43 J1–J14, V52/V53 F1–F12 and V55 physiological families.
13. Automated Playwright covers representative critical paths plus visual/human review. Technical PASS alone cannot close A63.
14. Final prototype acceptance becomes input to clean runtime materialization; no parallel permanent prototype architecture.

## Deliberate non-goals before Pauli

Do not perfect every animation, produce native mobile/wearable clients, build a full media platform, add social-network mechanics, create exhaustive localization, or over-model future ontology. The embryo is conceptually complete, operationally minimal, experientially extraordinary.
